Countact Dispenser

A two-sided dispenser for sealed five-pack contact lens strips. Press L or R for one strip; the count for that eye updates on the device and in the app.

What it does

  • ✓
    Separate left and right holders
    Identical, full-height and removable, so each eye's prescription stays in its own stack.
  • ✓
    One press, one sealed strip
    A strip pusher moves only the bottom five-pack forward; a retaining gate holds the rest.
  • ✓
    Counts only confirmed removals
    Two opposing optical sensors per outlet confirm the strip was taken before the count changes.
  • ✓
    Strips and lenses, per eye
    The display and companion app show strips left and lenses left (one strip = five lenses).
  • ✓
    Low-stock alerts
    Set an alert level for each side in the app.
