Meet Countact

Built around the strips you already use

Your connected five-pack blisters stack flat in a full-height holder for each eye. Press L or R and Countact pushes the bottom strip out through its outlet, foil seals intact.

How it works

Three steps, and the count only changes when a strip is really taken

1

Press L or R

Dispenses one sealed five-pack strip from that eye's holder.

2

Remove the strip

Take the dispensed strip from the outlet. Nothing is cut or opened.

3

Sensors confirm, count updates

Optical sensors detect the strip was taken and update inventory: one strip = five lenses (e.g. Left 6 → 5 strips, 30 → 25 lenses).

Questions

Good to know

Is Countact a finished product?

No. Countact is a concept in development. The mechanism and dimensions shown are proposals that still need prototype testing.

Which lens brands fit?

Countact is designed around connected five-pack strips of roughly 150 × 45 × 8 mm. Fit with specific brands has not been verified yet, so check back as testing progresses.

Does Countact open the lens packaging?

No. It dispenses whole sealed strips; the foil seals stay closed until you open a blister yourself.

Can I buy contact lenses here?

No. This store sells the dispenser only. The app's reorder button links to an outside lens retailer.
