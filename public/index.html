The everyday problem

Left and right don’t run out together.

Two eyes, two prescriptions, two supplies. Boxes pile up, you count by hand, and the last pair shows up as a surprise. Countact gives each eye its own holder and a running count.

  • One holder for each eye, so strips never mix.
  • One sealed strip per press.
  • A count for each eye that updates itself.
How it works

Four steps, one count per eye.

This is how Countact is intended to work. It is a proposed design, not a tested mechanism.

  1. 1 · Load
  2. 2 · Press
  3. 3 · Take
  4. 4 · Count

Fill each holder

Sealed strips go into the left and right holders. They never mix.

Press L or R

One button per eye releases one sealed strip.

Take the strip

The foil stays sealed. Optical sensors are proposed to confirm the strip was taken.

The count updates

Left goes from 5 to 4 strips, or 25 to 20 lenses. Right stays put.

FAQ

Good to know.

Which lens brands fit?

Countact is designed for connected five-pack strips, about 150 × 45 × 8 mm. Fit with specific brands hasn’t been verified.

Does it open the packaging?

No. You get the whole sealed strip, and the foil stays shut until you open it.

Can I buy lenses here?

No, just the dispenser. The app’s reorder button takes you to whichever lens shop you like.
